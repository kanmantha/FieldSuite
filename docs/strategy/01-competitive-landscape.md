# 01-Competitive-Landscape

October 2026

## Objective
To map FieldSuite's five bundled modules against incumbent vendors in the safety, quality, permits, estimation and light HR categories, plus relevant all-in-one suites. The focus is to identify gaps and weaknesses an SME-focused, modular, flat-bundled offering can exploit.

## 1. Safety & EHS
| Vendor | Pricing (as of Oct 2026) | Target Segment | Key Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| SafetyCulture/iAuditor | Free ≤10 users (5 templates). Premium $24/user/mo annual ($29 monthly). Enterprise contact-sales. AI credits metered on top of seats. | SMB → Enterprise | Seat-heavy once >10, AI credits metered, template caps on free. |
| EHS Insight | SMB from $5k/yr, full pricing contact-sales. | SMB/Industrial | Non-transparent, high entry for small firms, quote-only. |
| SiteDocs | Quote-only, priced by company size. | SMB contractors | Demo-gated, opaque TCO. |
| Fieldwire (Safety modules) | Free (5 users/3 projects). Pro $39, Business $64, Business Plus $89/user/mo annual. | GCs/subs | Modular safety is a subset; not a full EHS (incidents/toolbox talks depth), grows per user. |
| SafetyAmp | Champion from $4,800/yr site-based (not per-user). | Mid–Enterprise (multi-site) | Site-based minimum high for SME (<20), not per-user flexible. |
| Procore Safety | Contact-sales (~$4.5k–10k+/yr min, scales with construction volume). | Enterprise GC | Volume-based gating, onboarding/demo-gated, overkill for <50. |

**Gap:** Affordable, full incident/toolbox talk/near-miss + audit readiness without $4.8k site minimums or metered AI credits.

## Gap Summary (per vertical)
- Safety/EHS: AI metered + seat creep + $4.8k site minimums.
- QC/Snagging: project/user caps, regional opacity, fragmented from permits/safety.
- ePTW: Least transparent; forms vs lifecycle; contact-gated bands/minimums.
- Estimation: AI paywalled (Bluebeam Max), add-ons (Buildxact), per-license floor (PlanSwift).
- Light HR: thin integration to compliance/permits, admin fees, ERP dependencies.
- Suites: Volume/pricing opacity, implementation-heavy (Odoo), BIM-heavy (Autodesk), GC-centric (Procore) — total cost of ownership erodes SME budgets.
