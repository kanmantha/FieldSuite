Quality

Snags, inspections and NCRs

Purpose

Keep quality issues visible and closed out: the snag / punch list tracks defects to re-verification, inspections record structured checklist results, and NCRs capture non-conformances formally.

Key functions

  • Snag / punch list — raise with priority (including critical), assign, track status through to retest and close.
  • Inspections — build a checklist, record pass/fail per item, finalize the inspection.
  • NCRs — raise non-conformance reports and move them through a status workflow.
  • Work is linked to the responsible project, with owner and due date.
  • Notifications to the team when items are raised or assigned.

How to use it

  1. In Quality, choose Snag, Inspections or NCRs.
  2. Create the record with project, priority and assignee.
  3. Progress it through the status transitions as work is done.
Tip: use the AI Assistant’s Draft feature to convert a rough note into a structured snag/NCR entry, then review and save it here.
