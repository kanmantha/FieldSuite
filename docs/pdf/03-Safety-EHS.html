Incidents, corrective actions and toolbox talks

Purpose

Capture safety events the moment they happen, drive corrective actions to completion, and demonstrate a proactive safety culture with recorded toolbox talks.

Key functions

  • Report incidents & near-misses with category and severity.
  • Status lifecycle moves a report from open through action to closure.
  • Corrective actions — each incident can carry multiple actions with an owner and due date; overdue items alert on the dashboard.
  • Toolbox talks — record topic, date and facilitator; keep a history for audits.
  • Role-based notifications to Safety Officer / Site Manager.

How to use it

  1. Open Incidents & Near-Misses, click Report and fill in the details.
  2. Add corrective actions with assignee and due date.
  3. Transition status as work progresses; close once verified.
  4. Record toolbox talks under their tab.
Good practice: assign a due date to every corrective action — the platform flags overdue items automatically so they are never forgotten.
