FieldSuite — User Guide

Safety, quality, permits, estimation and workforce operations in one place

About FieldSuite

FieldSuite is a web-based operations platform for construction SMEs. It brings safety, quality, permitting, estimation and workforce data together per project, so every team works from the same live record. Each organization has its own isolated workspace, and all activity is audit-tracked.

Signing in

  1. Open your FieldSuite URL and go to the Sign in page.
  2. Enter your email and password, then click Sign in.
Demo access: on the hosted demo, pick a role card on the sign-in page to auto-fill credentials (any of the five demo accounts) and enter the password Demo123!.

Your workspace

  • Sidebar — navigate between modules; highlights the page you are on.
  • Page title bar — shows the active module at all times.
  • Notifications bell — unread alerts (e.g. overdue actions, new approvals) with a count badge.
  • User chip (top right) — your name, role, and Sign out.
  • Dashboard — a cross-module snapshot with key numbers and automated “Smart Nudges”.

Working role overview

RoleTypical capabilities
AdminFull access across every module and workspace configuration.
Site ManagerAll modules; submits and approves permits, manages workforce back office.
Safety OfficerIncidents, corrective actions and toolbox talks; permit approval/suspension.

EstimatorCreates and edits estimates and BOQ line items; suggests and applies rates.

Note: details can vary per site configuration — the sidebar only shows modules you can use, and guarded actions simply hide for other roles.

Working smarter

  • Smart Nudges on the dashboard flag overdue corrective actions, expiring documents, permits awaiting approval and more — with a one-click Review link.
  • Notifications keep assignees and approving roles informed without email.
  • AI Assistant answers questions using your own data, and drafts incident/snag/NCR entries from plain text for review before saving. It works offline (rules-based) by default.
  • Times are stored and displayed in UTC to avoid site/office time-zone confusion.
  • Print to PDF — details and proposal pages are print-friendly (use your browser’s Print / Save as PDF).
