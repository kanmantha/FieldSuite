Workforce

Attendance, workers and contractors

Purpose

Track who is on site, keep a worker register with their documents, and manage the contractor list — so staffing and compliance data are always current.

Key functions

  • Attendance — mark daily attendance per worker and record clock-outs; day view shows who is present.
  • Workers — worker register with details and a document store.
  • Documents — attach certifications / IDs with expiry dates; expiring documents are flagged on the dashboard.
  • Contractors — maintain the active contractors register.
  • Attendance today feeds the dashboard “Present today” KPI.

How to use it

  1. Under Workforce, keep Workers current (add documents with expiry dates).
  2. Each day, open Attendance and mark workers present / clock-out.
  3. Review Contractors as agreements start or end.
Tip: add a valid-to date on every worker document — FieldSuite warns you on the dashboard before anything expires.
